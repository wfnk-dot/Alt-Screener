# Dorito study: invalidation levels and lower-timeframe entries

49 coins, about 3.1 years of Binance 15-minute candles, generated 2026-11-13 15:04 UTC. "Last year" = from 13 Nov 2025.

**How to read it.** Numbers in brackets are the 95% range: with this many examples the true value is very likely inside it. ⚠️ = fewer than 100 examples, don't lean on it. Stops are 0.2% past the level; fees of 0.10% a trade are taken off. A trade still open after 20 Dorito-timeframe candles is closed at market.

**Trade test entry:** after the Dorito candle closes, the first lower-timeframe candle (within 3 Dorito candles) that closes with the XO bands the Dorito's way: red for tops, green for bottoms. If the level breaks before that, no trade. **No-Dorito baseline:** the same band-flip entries at random times with no Dorito nearby, stop past the swing high/low of the last 3 Dorito-timeframe candles. If a Dorito setup doesn't beat that, the Dorito isn't adding anything.

**Given back** = how much of the run into the Dorito had already been retraced by the candle close (a quick wick that dumped straight back = a lot given back).

## Setups that pass every check

Checked 200 combinations. To pass: 100+ trades, average result above 0 even at the low end of its range, still positive on the last year alone, and better than the no-Dorito baseline. With this many combinations a few could still pass by luck, so treat a lone pass with care.

**None.** Nothing cleared the bar.
