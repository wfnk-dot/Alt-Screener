# Dorito study: invalidation levels and entries

99 coins, about 3.0 years of Binance 15-minute candles, generated 2026-11-14 13:44 UTC. "Last year" = from 14 Nov 2025.

**Entries tested** (stop always just past the Dorito level, 0.2% buffer; fees of 0.10% a trade taken off; still open after 20 candles = closed):
- **at close**: buy/sell when the Dorito candle closes.
- **limit 50% back / 75% back**: after the close, a limit order half / three-quarters of the way back to the level, live for 3 candles. Only counts if it fills.
- **early (half-way)**: the Dorito is showing half-way through the candle: enter then, stop past the candle's low/high so far. Includes the ones that then disappear before the close.
- **15m flip etc.**: first lower-timeframe candle (within 3 candles) closing with the XO bands the Dorito's way.

**No-Dorito baseline**: the same entries at random candles with no Dorito nearby, using the swing low/high of the last 3 candles as the level. If a Dorito entry doesn't beat its baseline, the Dorito isn't adding anything.

**How to read it.** Brackets / ± = the 95% range. ⚠️ = fewer than 100 trades, don't lean on it. **Avg R** = average result per trade with a 3R target. Break-even with a 3R target needs about 25% of trades to reach 3R.

## Setups that pass every check

Checked 496 combinations. To pass: 100+ trades, average result above 0 even at the low end of its range, still positive on the last year alone, and better than the no-Dorito baseline. With this many combinations a few could pass by luck, so treat a lone pass with care.

- **3-day tops, 4h flip, All**: +0.35R a trade, 145 trades
- **4h tops, 1h flip, Given back <30%**: +0.15R a trade, 464 trades
- **4h bottoms, 1h flip, Bands above (Daily) green**: +0.13R a trade, 425 trades
- **4h tops, 1h flip, Bands above (Daily) red**: +0.10R a trade, 630 trades
- **4h bottoms, early (half-way), Bands above (Daily) red**: +0.09R a trade, 2240 trades
