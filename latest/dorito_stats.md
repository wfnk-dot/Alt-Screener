# Dorito stats: can we get in before the close?

50 coins (top by Hyperliquid volume), about 3.1 years of Binance 1-hour candles, generated 2026-11-13 13:38 UTC.

How to read it: **Showing at 50%** = the Dorito was on the chart halfway through the candle. **Still there at close** = how often it then confirmed. **Caught this early** = what share of all the Doritos that confirmed you'd have seen by then.

\* **Move left** = median move in the Dorito's direction (up for bottoms, down for tops) from the alert price to the close 3 candles after the Dorito candle, counting the ones that got cancelled. "Up" = how often that move was in your favour. **If you wait for the close** = the same, for the ones that confirmed, but entering at the close like today's alerts. **Price vs level** = how far price was from the candle's low (bottoms) or high (tops) at the alert.

Medians, not averages, so one huge candle doesn't skew it. Past results, not a promise.
